# EmpSphere

## Employee Operations Management System

EmpSphere is a web-based Employee Operations Management System designed to streamline workforce operations within an organization. The application focuses on employee administration, attendance monitoring, leave processing, task allocation, and department management through a centralized platform.

---

## Project Objectives

- Simplify employee record management
- Automate attendance and leave workflows
- Improve task tracking and operational efficiency
- Maintain structured departmental organization
- Provide analytical insights through dashboards

---

# Core Modules

1. Authentication & Authorization
- Secure Admin and Employee login
- Role-based access control
- Session management

2. Employee Management
- Add, update, and remove employee records
- Employee profile management
- Employee search and filtering

 3. Attendance Management
- Daily check-in/check-out tracking
- Attendance history and reports
- Late entry monitoring

4. Leave Management
- Leave request submission
- Approval and rejection workflow
- Leave status tracking

 5. Task Management
- Task assignment and monitoring
- Priority and deadline management
- Task progress tracking

 6. Department Management
- Department creation and management
- Employee allocation by department
- Department-wise employee overview

### 7. Dashboard & Analytics
- Employee statistics
- Attendance summaries
- Pending leave requests
- Active task overview
